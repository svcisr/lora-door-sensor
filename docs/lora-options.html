Quick picks

How the numbers are made

  • Airtime: Semtech LoRa formula, explicit header, CRC on; low-data-rate optimisation switches on automatically when a symbol is ≥16 ms (SF11/SF12 at 125 kHz, SF12 at 250 kHz).
  • TX current: whole board while transmitting. 22 dBm is measured at the battery: 140 mA with the Otii supplying 3.7 V into the TPS63802. The other powers are the SX1262 datasheet current with optimal PA settings (which RadioLib uses: 102 mA at 20 dBm, 95 at 17, 90 at 14) plus ~20 mA for the awake STM32; the 10 dBm radio figure (55 mA) is an estimate, the datasheet does not list it.
  • Charge per TX: TX current × airtime, plus the STM32 awake at ~20 mA for 12 ms around the packet, plus a fixed 20 ms LED flash (~2 mA).
  • Average current: charge per TX ÷ interval + 31 µA sleep floor, measured at the battery with the Otii supplying 3.7 V into the TPS63802 (STM32 STOP mode, SX1262 warm sleep; CH340, POW LED resistor and AMS1117 removed).
  • Sensitivity: −174 + 10·log(BW) + 6 dB noise figure + the SF's demodulation SNR limit (−7.5 dB at SF7 down to −20 dB at SF12).
  • Range: link budget minus a 10 dB fade margin. Open = line of sight over flat ground, antennas at 1.5 m (two-ray model). Urban = through buildings and walls (path-loss exponent 4). Treat both as ±50% estimates; a site test decides.

Not included, and limits

  • Board overhead: a stock DX-PJ26 sleeps at ~30 mA (CH340 on the 3.3 V rail, POW LED, AMS1117 back-fed from 3V3). The sleep floor above needs all three removed.
  • Measured vs. model at SF10 / 22 dBm: 0.29 s on air and ~42 mAs per packet on the board (the table gives ~41). The 140 mA and 31 µA are battery currents at 3.7 V through the TPS63802, about 3% below the 3.3 V board current; a lower cell voltage raises the battery current somewhat.
  • Door closed: the PB13 pull-up adds ~80 µA while the reed contact is closed.
  • 22 dBm needs ≥3.3 V on the radio; maximum power drops as the battery voltage falls.
  • Duty cycle: European 433 MHz rules allow 10 mW ERP and 10% duty cycle. Rows over 10% are flagged; anything above 10 dBm is over the usual power limit.
  • Link timeout: KEY selects the sensor heartbeat (2 s / 10 s / 1 min) and the receiver link timeout (7 s / 33 s / 190 s); set both boards of a pair to the same mode. Type the heartbeat into the interval box above to see its battery life.
  • Both boards must use the same SF and bandwidth. Change them in include/config.h.
